Cast

Each sprite is procedural: a body outline per species, a seeded granule field that rides with the body, a thin bright pellicle rim, food vacuoles, a darker macronucleus and a flickering cilia halo. The same anatomy is rendered as a dark rim and grey interior under brightfield. Sizes in the sheet are per-species; the atlas carries each one's µm per pixel.

Photographs: Wikimedia Commons, each credited with its licence. Hover a note for what the sprite copies.

Simulated clips

Agent-based behaviour from ecosim.py: Paramecium spiral, bump and back off; Didinium hunt, strike, swallow and digest; Stentor snaps shut when something passes its bell; rotifers hold on; Lacrymaria whips its neck out; a slow rotating convection current moves the debris and every free swimmer. Deterministic per seed, with ground-truth tracks and an event log.

The loop: cheat sheet → footage → tracks → cheat sheet

species.py holds the numbers (speed, turn rate, strike radius, contraction time) in micrometres and seconds. ecosim.py turns them into footage. track.py turns footage into tracks. trackstats.py turns tracks back into the same numbers, convection subtracted, as a file ecosim.py --params accepts. Today the loop is closed on synthetic data; once the jar delivers real swimmers and G4 labels, the fitted file replaces the typed one.

How the sprites are built

Outline: Paramecium is an ellipse with a rounder anterior; Didinium a superellipse barrel with a snout and two ciliary girdles; Stentor a half-width profile that widens as u1.5 to a bell and collapses to a ball at contraction 1 (length × 0.25); rotifer a spindle with a foot and two corona discs that pulse in antiphase; Lacrymaria a teardrop with a quadratic-Bézier neck in pixels; Dileptus an ellipse over the rear two thirds plus a tapering trunk; Daphnia an oval with a tail spine, an eye and two antennae; amoeba and debris are radial noise.

Shading: mask minus its erosion is the rim; a band-limited noise field, seeded per organism so it never swims against the body, is the granule texture; dilation minus mask, blurred and flickered by a per-frame RNG, is the cilia halo. Darkfield intensity = 0.3·interior + 0.9·rim + halo + vacuoles − nucleus. Brightfield darkness = 0.5·interior + 0.7·rim + nucleus − vacuoles. The sprite returns both maps; the compositor takes the max over a dark field or the min under a bright one.
